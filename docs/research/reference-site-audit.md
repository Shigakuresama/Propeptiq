# Reference-Site and Catalog Taxonomy Audit

**Observed in Chrome:** 2026-08-24.

**Purpose:** Identify transferable information architecture and public competitor taxonomy without treating competitor content as PROPEPTIQ catalog evidence.

## Provn (`provnrx.com`)

Provn is a telehealth and online-prescription experience. Its public navigation and home page organize around human outcomes such as Weight Loss, Strength, Anti-Aging, Sexual Wellness, and More. Public product examples included prescription/compounded offerings and human-directed claims.

### Transferable presentation patterns

- Restrained off-white/black/sage palette.
- Clear two-column hero and direct primary action.
- Generous whitespace, compact pill controls, simple grids, and a dark footer reversal.
- Technical/legal material separated from the main content hierarchy.

### Prohibited carryover

The human-use taxonomy, product names, prices, imagery, trust claims, provider/pharmacy language, outcome copy, shipping claims, assets, and branded styling are not reusable. PROPEPTIQ must not resemble a telehealth, prescription, weight-management, sexual-wellness, strength, longevity, or anti-aging service.

## Amino Club (`aminoclub.com`)

The public catalog presented category filters named All, Sprays, Tissue Repair, Dermal, Metabolic, Secretagogue, Cellular, Neuro, and Circadian.

Public product names observed included:

- GLP-3 (RT), GLP-2 (TR), GLP-1 (SM), Tesamorlin, Cagrilintide, AOD-9604, 5-Amino-1MQ.
- BPC-157, TB-500, BPC-157/TB-500, KPV, KLOW, GLOW, ARA-290, LL-37, Cartalax, Thymosin Alpha-1, Sermorelin, Kisspeptin, VIP, and Pinealon.
- GHK-Cu, AHK-Cu, SNAP-8, Melanotan I, and Melanotan II.
- CJC-1295/Ipamorelin, Ipamorelin, IGF-1 LR3, MOTS-C, NAD+, and Glutathione.
- SEMAX, SELANK, DSIP, Dihexa, and Epithalon.
- Spray variants publicly labeled GHK-Cu, NAD+, SEMAX, SELANK, PT-141, Melanotan II, DSIP, BPC-157, and BPC-157/TB-500, plus other public spray names.

This is a point-in-time competitor observation, not a recommendation or PROPEPTIQ SKU list. Names, classifications, availability, and legality require independent verification.

### Transferable structural patterns

- Catalog filters separate category from sort order.
- A product record gives the lot/COA relationship first-class space.
- Current and historical lots are distinguishable.
- Technical specifications are structured fields rather than buried prose.
- COA access and analytical methods can be placed near the relevant lot.
- The public navigation separates Products, Bulk Orders, COAs, and account access.

### Patterns rejected for PROPEPTIQ

- Public purchase without verified researcher/organization approval.
- Promotions, countdowns, subscriptions, bundles, quantity discounts, “frequently researched together” cross-sells, or urgency copy before catalog/legal approval.
- Unverified purity badges, laboratory/accreditation claims, shipping dates, stock, prices, or guarantees.
- Therapeutic-adjacent category labels or compound descriptions.
- A checkbox or disclaimer as the sole preventive measure.
- Human/veterinary research summaries that could establish intended human use.

## PROPEPTIQ catalog decision

No competitor category or product is seeded into the application. The initial public catalog must be a designed empty state until an approved catalog manifest supplies verified records. The data model supports categories, products, lots, methods, COAs, and per-jurisdiction decisions without hardcoding any of them.

## Copyright and trademark boundary

Do not hotlink or copy reference-site images, logos, PDFs, text, CSS, video, product renders, lab marks, or distinctive branded compositions. Abstract layout observations may inform a new original system. Production imagery must be owned/licensed and tied to an approved product record.
